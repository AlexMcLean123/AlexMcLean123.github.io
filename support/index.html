Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ciorcal?

Ciorcal is a community app for expats and newcomers. It helps you find events, housing, connect with people, and settle into your new city. The name comes from the Irish word for "circle".

Is Ciorcal free?

Yes, Ciorcal is free to download and use. All core features including events, housing, messaging, community feed, and the Buy Nothing raffle are available at no cost.

How does the Buy Nothing raffle work?

When a member lists an item they no longer need, other users can enter to receive it. Ciorcal automatically runs a fair raffle and selects a winner. Everything is free — no payment or exchange required.

Where is Ciorcal available?

Ciorcal is currently live in Vancouver, Canada. We plan to expand to more cities worldwide. The app is available on iOS, with Android coming soon.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the Settings page within the app. Alternatively, email us at support@ciorcal.io and we will process your request within 30 days.

How do I report a user or inappropriate content?

You can report users or content directly within the app by tapping the menu on any post or profile. You can also email us at support@ciorcal.io with details and we will investigate promptly.

I'm having trouble signing in

Try resetting your password from the sign-in screen. If you signed up with Apple or Google, make sure you're using the same method. If you're still having trouble, contact us at support@ciorcal.io.
