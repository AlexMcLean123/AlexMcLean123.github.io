Privacy Policy

Ciorcal ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ciorcal mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. By using Ciorcal, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, profile photo, and password.
  • Profile information: Bio, location, nationality, and other details you choose to share.
  • User-generated content: Posts, messages, comments, photos, and other content you submit to the community feed, chat, or housing listings.
  • Event data: Events you create, attend, or express interest in.
  • Buy Nothing data: Items you list, raffle entries, and related interactions.
  • Communications: Messages you send to other users through the App.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers.
  • Usage data: Features used, interactions, time spent, and navigation patterns.
  • Log data: IP address, access times, and crash reports.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party services you use to sign in, such as Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, including your name and email address as permitted by your settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features.
  • Create and manage your account.
  • Enable communication between users.
  • Facilitate events, housing listings, and the Buy Nothing raffle system.
  • Send notifications related to your account, events, messages, and community activity.
  • Track rewards and badges for community participation.
  • Monitor and enforce our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Analyse usage trends to improve the user experience.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Your profile information, posts, event attendance, and Buy Nothing listings are visible to other Ciorcal users as part of the community experience.
  • Service providers: We use third-party services (such as Firebase by Google) for authentication, data storage, analytics, and push notifications. These providers only access your data as necessary to perform their functions.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.
  • Safety: We may share information if we believe it is necessary to protect the safety of our users or the public.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure with industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. While we implement reasonable safeguards, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@ciorcal.io.

7. Children's Privacy

Ciorcal is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 17, we will take steps to delete that information.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including Canada and the United States. These countries may have different data protection laws. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy in the App or by email. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us
